Guided Learning Starts With a Story

Brightpath was built by educators who believed tutoring could be more personal, more measurable, and a lot more encouraging.

Our Story

From a Kitchen Table to a Full Learning Center

Brightpath Tutoring Center opened its doors in 2014, founded by a former public-school teacher who was frustrated watching capable students fall behind simply because thirty-student classrooms couldn't slow down for the one or two concepts each child was still missing. What started as after-school sessions with three families around a kitchen table has grown into a dedicated K-12 learning center serving Riverdale and the surrounding community.

Today, Brightpath supports more than 1,200 families across Primary, Middle and High School — but the founding idea hasn't changed: every student learns differently, and the right tutor, teaching the right way, at the right pace, changes everything.

Our Mission

To help every student build both the academic skills and the personal confidence to own their education — not just pass the next test.

Our Approach

We diagnose before we teach, match tutors by subject expertise rather than availability, and track progress with real attendance, homework and test data — not just a report card at the end of term.

Founder & Director

Anjali Desai

M.Ed. Curriculum & Instruction · 15 years in education

"I spent nine years teaching in public classrooms before starting Brightpath, and the question that never left me was: what happens to the student who just needs five more minutes? Brightpath is my answer — a place where that student always gets those five minutes, and a tutor who actually has the subject expertise to give them well."
