How Our Team Teaches

Our Teaching Methods

Every tutor brings their own style, but the whole team is trained around these shared principles.

Diagnostic-First

Every tutor starts with a short assessment to see exactly where a student is, not where the syllabus assumes they are.

Hands-On & Project-Based

Science, coding and languages lean on real experiments, projects and conversation practice, not just worksheets.

Socratic Discussion

English and Social Studies tutors ask guiding questions instead of handing over answers, building independent thinking.

Visual & Multisensory

Diagrams, manipulatives and color-coding help concepts stick for visual and hands-on learners alike.

Exam Strategy Coaching

Test-prep and AP-track tutors drill pacing, question triage and timed practice, not just raw content review.

Progress You Can See

Every method above is paired with attendance, homework and test tracking in the parent dashboard.
